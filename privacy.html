Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Light Lamp Studios ("we", "us") handles information in our games and on lightlampstudios.com. Light Lamp Studios is the business name of Knighthunter Cook, a sole proprietor based in Washington State, USA. This policy covers every game published under the Light Lamp Studios developer name on Google Play, including Butter & Bloom, Rust & Revive, Crown & Facet, Tidewell Aquatics, Deep Time and Backyard Bloom.

The short version

  • Our games do not collect, store or share your personal information.
  • Our games contain no ads, no ad tracking and no third-party analytics.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your own device.
  • Purchases and free trials are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information our games collect

None. Our games do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and they do not create accounts. We do not sell, rent or share personal information because we don't collect it.

Game saves

Your progress, settings and preferences are saved locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Purchases, free trials and Google Play

Our games are sold through Google Play. Payments, refunds, free trials and the related account information are handled by Google, not by us. We never see your payment details. Google's use of information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Google Play may also give developers anonymous, aggregated statistics (such as total installs or crash reports) that do not identify you personally. We use them only to fix bugs and improve our games.

Permissions

Our games only use what they need to run, such as vibration for haptic feedback. They do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Playtesting and emails

If you email us, for example to join a playtest or ask for support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply to you and, for playtesters, to add your Google account to the test on Google Play. We never sell or share it, and we'll delete it if you ask.

This website

lightlampstudios.com is a simple website hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children's privacy

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. Because our games collect no personal information, no personal information from children is collected.

Changes to this policy

If our games ever change how they handle information, for example by adding online features, we will update this page and the effective date above before that change goes live.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your privacy? Email hello@lightlampstudios.com.
