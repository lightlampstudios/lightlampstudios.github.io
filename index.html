Play it before anyone else

Playtesters get a free copy on Android and help decide what changes before a game launches. It takes about 20 minutes per test.

Email to join the playtest
  1. Send your Gmail address. Use the one signed in to Google Play on your Android phone.
  2. Accept the invite. You'll get a link and a free code to install the test build from Google Play.
  3. Play, then tell me how it felt. A one-page form with room for screenshots. Honest is better than nice.

A small studio with the light on

Light Lamp Studios started with a simple idea: games should feel good. Not stressful, not pushy, just satisfying. The click of a screw coming loose, a perfect frosting swirl, the first clean pane of an aquarium.
