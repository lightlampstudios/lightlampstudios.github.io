Who is Light Lamp?

A small studio with a warm light on

Light Lamp Studios started with a simple idea: games should feel good. Not stressful, not pushy, just satisfying. The click of a screw coming loose, a perfect frosting swirl, the first clean pane of an aquarium.

I'm a solo developer who works in IT by day and builds games by lamplight at night. Every game is designed, tested and polished by hand, and shaped by real players in playtests before it ever reaches the store.

Knighthunter Thorin Cook, founder

  • Built for Android phones and tablets, and PC through Google Play Games.
  • Saves automatically, so you never lose your progress.
  • Relaxing music with full volume control, and menus that stay out of the way.
  • Tested from start to finish on real devices before every release.
  • No ads, no data selling and no tricks. Just a good game at a fair price.
